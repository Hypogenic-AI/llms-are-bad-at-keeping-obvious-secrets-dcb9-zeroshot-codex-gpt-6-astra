# Protocol fixed before behavioral outcome inspection
Date: 2026-10-02. See design.json for all prompts and seeds.
Primary writer: OpenRouter google/gemma-3-12b-it, temperature 0.9, cap 1100 tokens, requested 450 words, user-message secret.
Word design: 120 independently generated pairs (15 target words x 8 repetitions), four paired conditions plain, filler, outline, decoy. Same outline and decoy within a pair. Comparator secrets assigned by cyclic offsets, avoiding identical secrets. 12 prewritten outlines, constructed without target words as inputs. Outlines can incidentally associate with secrets; reuse within paired conditions and secret pairs prevents a direct label shortcut.
Plot design: 20 authored premises with two future facts each x 3 repetitions x plain/filler/outline/no_secret. Paired texts share premise; outline is a generic six-beat opening plan, independent of twist. No-secret labels have no relation to prompt content. These stimuli test withheld future facts, not necessarily literary surprise.
Primary reader: anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6. Secondary reader: openai/gpt-4.1-mini. Two independent requests per pair, reversed text order, temperature 0. Parse A or B. Average correctness over orders before inference. Report order agreement and position preference. No guesser sees condition labels, outlines, or private prompts.
Primary contrasts: outline minus plain and outline minus filler in word and plot domains. Paired block bootstrap, plus item-cluster bootstrap sensitivity (target word or plot premise), 95% intervals. Two-sided paired sign-flip tests, Holm adjustment across four primary contrasts. Report signed discrimination, not interpret near chance as proof of privacy. Decoy and no-secret are anchors. Do not treat order duplicates as independent n.
Exploratory local mechanism: same checkpoint family, exact revision saved. Difference directions from paired secret vs no-secret prompts under identical teacher-forced neutral continuations, held-out continuations for validation. Sample early/middle/late generated positions to assess secret-aligned activation; probe readout is not evidence of internal maintenance rather than prompt re-reading. Single-layer projection intervention, random and other-concept controls, generation quality assessment. Any mechanism experiment is exploratory and not confirmatory.

Mechanism details fixed before readout: four concrete words lighthouse/violin/cactus/telescope; first 24 available no-secret story IDs sorted and frozen, 12 train/12 test. Residual outputs at layers 12/24/36/48 and continuation tokens 64/192/384/576. Directions are per-secret means centered across the four secret means, not raw secret-vs-neutral differences (which would retain a generic secrecy instruction component). Intervention fixed at layer 24. Reference center from no-secret training residuals. Remove target projection at the final prefill position and each subsequent generated position; prompt KV entries are retained. Random and next-word directions orthogonalized to the target, with identical per-state subtraction magnitude. Six repetitions x four target words = 24 independently generated pairs per intervention, 192 local stories total. Interventions use the same batch seed; this is common random-number coupling, not guaranteed token alignment after EOS. Local generation cap 900 tokens. No ablation strength tuning on outcomes.
Technical amendment before any readout: one initial neutral story had fewer than 576 tokens, causing the extraction to stop. Freeze the first 24 available no-secret stories with at least 576 tokens instead; preserve the initial source manifest and error log. This is a length eligibility filter only, not an outcome filter.
Post-readout exploratory diagnostics: the raw direction classifier performed poorly. Report two explicitly labeled sensitivities using the already saved held-out states: subtract the corresponding no-secret continuation state, and subtract the mean state across all four secret counterfactuals of that same continuation. The latter is an oracle contrast, not a deployable probe. Do not retune intervention direction or layer based on these diagnostics. Correlate baseline pair-averaged normalized target projection with pair discrimination using Spearman correlation; exploratory, no causal interpretation.
Tokenization diagnostic after readout: telescope spans two standalone Gemma tokens while the other three secrets span one. Add a three-way oracle-centered readout trained and tested on the equally tokenized subset; its chance level is one third. This tests whether the contrast signal can be explained solely by prompt length. It is a post hoc sensitivity, not a replacement of the four-way results.
Post hoc supervised readout: to test whether failure of the simple centroid decoder reflects absence of information or its noisy estimator, fit StandardScaler plus multinomial logistic regression (C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=3000) on all 12 training continuations, four secret labels, and four positions, separately at each saved layer. Fix settings without a hyperparameter search. Evaluate the same held-out continuations. At layer 24 also algebraically apply the already fixed target projection removal to held-out states and test whether the supervised probe still decodes the secret. This is a local readout diagnostic, not a downstream generation intervention or new confirmatory test.
